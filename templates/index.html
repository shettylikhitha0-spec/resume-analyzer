    System Modules & 3-Tier Architecture

    Detailed design implementation matching system specifications 6.1 through 6.6.

    6.1 Resume Upload Module

    Allows users to upload resumes in PDF format using PyPDF2 or paste raw text. Extracts text streams safely and sanitizes encoding.

    PyPDF2.PdfReader & io.BytesIO

    6.2 Job Description Module

    Enables intake of job postings, skill requirements, and benchmark profiles against which resumes are evaluated.

    Job Profile Intake & Presets

    6.3 Text Preprocessing Module

    Lowercases, strips punctuation, emails, and URLs, and eliminates NLTK English stopwords to retain dense technical tokens.

    NLTK Tokenizer & Stopwords

    6.4 Feature Extraction Module

    Converts textual content into numerical TF-IDF feature vectors capturing unigram and bigram keyword importance.

    sklearn.TfidfVectorizer

    6.5 Similarity & Scoring Module

    Calculates the mathematical angle between high-dimensional resume and job vectors to derive a normalized cosine similarity percentage.

    sklearn.cosine_similarity

    6.6 Skill Analysis & Ranking

    Extracts domain technical skills, highlights matched and missing capabilities, and ranks multiple candidate profiles with Pandas export.

    Pandas DataFrame & CSV Engine

    AI Resume Screening & Ranking System • Python, Scikit-Learn, NLTK, Pandas & Flask
